Hello, I’m Avery

I build useful things
and draw strange ones.

A multidisciplinary engineer and illustrator based in Brooklyn. I make thoughtful digital products, then refill my cup with color, characters, and a very good cup of coffee.

A little about me

Logic-minded.
Wonder-led.

My work lives where visual systems and human experience meet. For the past six years, I’ve designed and engineered web experiences for people who care about the details.

When I’m not untangling product problems, I’m making editorial illustrations and tiny worlds in my sketchbook. Both practices keep me asking better questions.
