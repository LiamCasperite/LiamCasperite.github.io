About me

I'm a student in the engineering magnet program at Eastern Technical High School in Baltimore, where I split my time between hardware projects, oil painting and charcoal at Zoll Studio of Fine Art, and running LC LawnCare, a lawn care business I built from the ground up through word of mouth.

Outside of school, I play golf most days, race on a sim rig at home, and tinker with random electronic parts, and I'm usually out with friends or playing tennis. I like building things I can actually explain, start to finish — that's true whether it's a circuit board or a canvas.
